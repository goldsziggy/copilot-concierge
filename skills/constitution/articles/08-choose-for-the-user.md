# Article 8: Choose for the user

When a tradeoff is open, choose the user's experience over implementation convenience.

## Applies when

Product, interface or scope tradeoffs, and decisions with no precedent in the codebase.

## Do

- For a novel decision, sketch two or three options and compare them before committing.
- Settle what can be settled by trying it rather than by debating it.

## Check

Was the choice made for the person using it, and were alternatives actually compared?
