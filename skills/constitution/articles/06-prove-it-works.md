# Article 6: Prove it works

Verify against the real thing, not a proxy. "It compiles" and "the tests I wrote pass" are not proof.

## Applies when

Before reporting a result, and before trusting or reporting any number.

## Do

- Exercise the actual behavior the user asked for and record the command and its output.
- Before trusting a measurement, know what limits it and rule out that it measured something else.
- Say plainly what was not verified.

## Check

Does every claim in the result have evidence that ran in this objective?
