# Article 9: Build the lever

Build the tool that does or proves the work, and turn a repeated instruction into structure.

## Applies when

Non-trivial or repetitive work, or writing the same rule a second time.

## Do

- Prefer a script, codemod or generator a reviewer can rerun over doing it by hand.
- Encode a rule you keep restating as a check, lint, test or SOP instead of more prose.

## Check

Can someone else rerun what you did, and will the lesson hold without anyone remembering it?
